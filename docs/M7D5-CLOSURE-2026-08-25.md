- reprocesses only events that required detail;
- does not refetch supplier detail during CEI processing;
- leaves MOVED-only/no-detail work out of CEI reprocessing;
- reapplies the durable canonical merchant override to the candidate effective view;
- persists candidate classification and generic intelligence under the matching private run;
- leaves canonical catalog, canonical CEI, merchant override truth and LKG untouched;
- leaves `supplier_sync_stage_runs.state=details_complete` for M7D6 verification ownership;
- leaves recurring Intelligent Sync disabled.
